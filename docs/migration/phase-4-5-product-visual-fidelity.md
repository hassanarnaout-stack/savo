## Deviations found and corrected

- Product-card image links were static parents of `next/image` fill images. The V21 absolute media-link geometry and responsive `sizes` were restored.
- Product cards lacked the source metadata row. Real `brandName`, rating, countdown, and order-count signals now occupy the V21 metadata/stock positions without invented values.
- The extra card savings line, absent from the V21 generic card, was removed.
- Locale-sensitive order-count rendering caused Arabic hydration mismatches. Counts now render deterministically.
- Prisma Decimal values in nested product relationships were not fully serialized. Recursive, value-preserving serialization removes Server/Client warnings without changing data.
- The PDP lacked the V21 savings, truthful Rescue, stock/progress, verified-supplier, and information-tab surfaces. These now use existing operational fields only.
- Gallery, thumbnail, product-card, and FBT fill images now provide appropriate `sizes`.
- Product surfaces previously referenced a nonexistent `/placeholder-product.png`, which produced a 404 and an invalid-image warning when operational data had no image. They now use the neutral, explicitly unavailable `/placeholder-product.svg`; no product image or product data was substituted.

## Intentional data-driven differences

- Operational names, Arabic names, images, prices, stock, badges, ratings, relationships, and merchandising state replace V21 demo content.
- Missing reviews do not fabricate ratings.
- Rescue treatment appears only for a real `RESCUE` product with an expiry date.
- No SAVO Plus claim is fabricated where the operational product does not expose verified eligibility.
- Buy Now and delivery promises were not invented. Existing operational checkout/cart behavior remains authoritative.

## Phase 4.6 drawer conflict

- Root cause: Mobile Navigation used isolated component state while Cart Drawer used persisted Zustand state. Neither surface notified the other when opening, and `isOpen` was persisted alongside cart contents, allowing a stale open cart to reappear after reload beneath the mobile navigation.
- Correction: opening either drawer emits a presentation-only coordination event that closes the other first. Cart persistence now contains cart items only and deliberately ignores legacy persisted `isOpen` values.
- Cart Drawer now matches Mobile Navigation safety behavior: `body` and `.store-scroll` are locked, the exact `.store-scroll` position is retained, Escape closes the drawer, focus is trapped inside, and focus returns to the cart trigger.
- Live result: Mobile Navigation open state showed one nav scrim and zero cart dialogs; Cart open state showed one cart overlay and zero nav scrims. Both restored scroll styles and trigger focus after Escape.
