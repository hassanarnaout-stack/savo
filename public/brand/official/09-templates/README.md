# SAVO Business and Marketing Template Specifications

No company address, telephone, email, legal name, tax number, bank data or QR code has been supplied. Every field below remains a clearly marked placeholder.

## Business card

Use the Black lockup on White or White lockup on Dark Navy. Maintain x clear space. Fields: `[FULL NAME]`, `[ROLE]`, `[PHONE]`, `[EMAIL]`, `[WEB]`, `[ADDRESS]`. Final size, bleed, stock and finish come from the printer.

## Letterhead and envelope

Place the lockup at the leading top edge with clear space. Keep legal/contact fields in a restrained footer. Printer supplies page/envelope size, window position, bleed and postal constraints.

## Invoice and quotation header

Use a compact lockup with document title, `[LEGAL ENTITY]`, `[REGISTRATION]`, `[TAX ID]`, `[CONTACT]`, document number/date and customer fields. Do not embed banking or tax claims until approved.

## Email signature

Use a small hosted PNG/SVG where the mail client permits it, plus live accessible text. Fields: `[NAME]`, `[ROLE]`, `[PHONE]`, `[EMAIL]`, `[WEBSITE]`. Avoid large banners and animated marks.

## Presentation

Cover: Ink field, White lockup, optional Teal rule, `[PRESENTATION TITLE]`, `[DATE]`. Interior: White/Surface field, Ink text, Teal for navigation, Fire only for urgency. Use Lexend Exa headings, Manrope English body and Cairo Arabic.

## Advertisement and flyer bases

Maintain a clear brand zone for the lockup, a content zone for approved campaign material and a legal/CTA zone. Do not invent claims, prices, imagery, QR codes or contact data. Output dimensions and bleed are channel/vendor inputs.
