# SAVO Brand Guidelines

Version 1.0 - approved identity system. The outlined master artwork is locked.

## 01 Brand Overview

SAVO is Kuwait's discovery-led commerce brand. Its approved positioning is **Your World of Discovery** / **عالمك للاكتشاف**. The identity should feel modern, confident, accessible and premium, while communicating value without looking cheap.

## 02 Logo

The primary identity combines the dark rounded-square S mark, Teal S, Fire dot and outlined AVO artwork. The authoritative source is `01-master/SAVO-LOGO-MASTER-OUTLINED.ai`. Never redraw or reset it.

## 03 Logo Variations

- Primary/light surface: `02-svg/SAVO-Logo-Primary.svg` or `SAVO-Logo-Black.svg`
- Dark surface: `02-svg/SAVO-Logo-White.svg`
- Compact applications: `02-svg/SAVO-Mark-FullColor.svg`
- The full-color mark is the approved secondary configuration.
- No approved monochrome standalone mark exists. Do not fabricate one.

All production vectors contain outlined artwork and no live text.

## 04 Clear Space

Let **x** equal 25% of the displayed S-mark width. Maintain at least x around every side of the complete lockup or standalone mark. No type, edge, image detail or trim may enter this area.

## 05 Minimum Size

- Digital lockup: 120 px wide minimum
- Digital mark: 16 px square absolute minimum; 32 px preferred
- Print lockup: 32 mm wide minimum
- Print mark: 8 mm square minimum

Use larger sizes for embroidery, dimensional signage and coarse substrates. Approve a physical sample near any minimum.

## 06 Logo Misuse

Do not stretch, squash, rotate, recolor, add shadows, add outlines, apply gradients, change AVO artwork, change S geometry, move the Fire dot, alter spacing, distort the corner radius, crop, separate or rearrange elements, place on low-contrast imagery, or recreate the logo with HTML/CSS/type.

## 08 Digital Color Specifications

Use the listed sRGB HEX/RGB values for screens. Preserve embedded sRGB profiles in raster exports. Do not sample approximate colors from screenshots or compressed images.

## 09 Print Color Guidance

The immutable master is RGB. No printer ICC profile, substrate or press condition was supplied; therefore no CMYK build is final. A production vendor must convert a duplicate using the agreed ICC profile and provide a contract or physical proof. Pantone/spot matches require physical swatch approval. See `04-print/CMYK-WORKFLOW.md`.

## 10 Typography

The outlined AVO is custom logo artwork; its originating typeface is unverified. Never typeset it. Application typography is acquired separately under the applicable font licenses; font files are not distributed in this kit.

## 11 English Typography

- Display: Lexend Exa 700-800
- H1: Lexend Exa 700-800
- H2: Lexend Exa 700
- H3: Lexend Exa 600-700
- Body Large: Manrope 500
- Body: Manrope 400-500
- Small/Caption: Manrope 400-600
- Button/CTA: Manrope 700

Use sentence case for interface text. Reserve all caps for short labels and urgency markers.

## 12 Arabic Typography

Use Cairo with `dir="rtl"` and natural right alignment where Arabic stands alone. Suggested hierarchy: headings 700, body 400-500, labels/CTAs 600-700. Do not mirror or reshape the SAVO logo.

## 13 Iconography

Use simple, geometric line icons with consistent stroke weight and optical size. Teal indicates active/discovery states; Fire is reserved for urgency and exceptional emphasis. Do not use the Fire dot as a generic bullet or decorative confetti.

## 14 Digital Usage

Use SVG for responsive website/app lockups, the standalone mark for square icons, and supplied PNGs only where vectors are unsupported. Preserve intrinsic aspect ratios and transparency. Header, mobile header, drawer and footer use the White lockup on dark surfaces.

## 15 Social Media

Use the full-color mark for avatars. Keep it centered with platform-safe padding. Base canvases and exact platform specifications are in `07-social/SOCIAL-ASSET-SPECS.md`. Templates intentionally contain no campaign copy.

## 16 Marketing Applications

Business and marketing masters must lead with the approved lockup, use the documented type system and palette, and keep supplied business fields as placeholders until authoritative details are provided. See `09-templates/`.

## 17 Packaging

Packaging must use supplier-issued dielines. Keep the lockup clear of folds, seams, closures and trim; approve white ink where required on transparent or dark substrates. Placement guidance is in `10-packaging/`; no dielines or dimensions are fabricated.

## 18 Physical and Office Branding

Preserve clear space and minimum reproduction across exterior signs, reception walls, decals, badges, uniforms and vehicles. Fabric, vinyl, paint and dimensional-letter samples require vendor proofing. See `11-office/`.

## 19 Brand Voice

### Principles

- **Discovery-driven:** reveal useful finds and meaningful value.
- **Confident:** clear, direct and specific; never boastful.
- **Accessible premium:** considered and polished, never exclusive or intimidating.
- **Value with dignity:** communicate savings without bargain-bin language.
- **Bilingual by design:** English and Arabic should feel native, not mechanically translated.

English headlines are concise, active and benefit-led. Arabic should use natural contemporary phrasing and preserve meaning rather than word order. CTAs use direct verbs such as Discover, Explore, Shop, Add to Cart and View Deals. Avoid hype without evidence, fake scarcity, luxury clichés, slang that dates quickly, and claims such as “best” or “guaranteed” without substantiation.

## 20 Printer Handoff

Use `04-print/SAVO-Logo-Master-Outlined-RGB.ai` or the supplied vector PDF. The RGB outlined master is immutable. Convert only a duplicate after confirming process, stock, substrate, ICC profile, black construction, trapping, bleed, overprint and white-ink needs. Require proofs before mass production.
