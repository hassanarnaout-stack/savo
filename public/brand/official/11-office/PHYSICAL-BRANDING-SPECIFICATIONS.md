# SAVO Office, Signage and Uniform Specifications

No physical dimensions are prescribed. Survey measurements, materials, mounting and local approvals come from the appointed vendor.

| Application | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Exterior sign | Use high-contrast lockup; engineer illumination, weathering and viewing distance |
| Interior wall/reception | Preserve clear space; dimensional letters must retain exact outlined geometry |
| Door/window decal | Use Black on light glazing treatment or White on dark treatment; assess daylight contrast |
| Employee badge | Use mark where lockup falls below minimum; keep names as live accessible type |
| Staff/delivery uniform | Use approved embroidery/transfer sample; increase size for coarse fabric |
| Vehicle branding | Place away from handles, panel gaps and legal markings; verify views on all sides |

Vendors must not redraw the mark, simplify paths, move the Fire dot or approximate colors. Require material samples and physical proofs for paint, vinyl, embroidery, screen print, dimensional letters and illuminated signs.
