# SAVO Pantone and Spot-Color Status

No Pantone or other spot-color assignment is approved. Do not select a match from an on-screen conversion.

When spot color is required, the brand owner, designer and printer must compare current physical coated/uncoated swatches under controlled lighting, produce a substrate/process drawdown, and approve the result in writing. Record the library, suffix, substrate, ink system and approval date.
