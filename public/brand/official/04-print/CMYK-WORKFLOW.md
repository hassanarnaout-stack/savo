# SAVO CMYK Production Workflow

The RGB outlined master is immutable. No authoritative CMYK build can be supplied without a press/substrate ICC profile.

1. Duplicate the approved RGB AI master and retain the original unchanged.
2. Confirm printing process, stock/substrate, coating, ink system and destination ICC profile.
3. Convert the duplicate through a color-managed workflow; do not manually guess separations.
4. Inspect total ink limit, black construction, overprint, trapping and white-ink requirements.
5. Supply a digital proof and, for color-critical work, a physical contract or production proof.
6. Record the ICC profile, press condition, substrate and approval date in the filename/job record.

Coated and uncoated paper require their respective profiles. Packaging depends on process and substrate. Fabric requires process-specific samples. Signage and large-format work require the device/media profile and viewing-light evaluation. Reference CMYK percentages are intentionally omitted because they would be non-authoritative without these inputs.
