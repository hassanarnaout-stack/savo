This package contains the approved SAVO outlined logo. Do not redraw, reset, recolor, stretch, squash, crop, rearrange or add effects to the artwork.

Preferred editable master:

`../01-master/SAVO-LOGO-MASTER-OUTLINED.ai`

`SAVO-Logo-Master-Outlined-RGB.pdf`

## CMYK and Pantone status

No approved CMYK build or Pantone assignment is supplied. Do not guess either value. Convert the RGB master using the agreed press ICC profile for the actual process, stock and substrate. Supply a proof showing the proposed conversion before production.

Pantone status: **Not assigned**.

## Clear space and minimum size

Clear space on every side must be at least 25% of the displayed S-mark width.

- Complete logo: minimum 32 mm wide in print
- Standalone mark: minimum 8 mm square in print

If production constraints require smaller reproduction, request written approval after reviewing a physical sample.

## Production requirements

- Keep the logo vector wherever possible.
- Do not rasterize the AI/PDF/SVG master.
- Do not substitute fonts or recreate AVO as text.
- Confirm bleed, trapping, overprint and black construction for the specific production process.
- Confirm whether white artwork requires white ink on transparent or colored substrates.
- Send a digital proof before mass production.
- Provide a physical color proof for packaging, shopping bags, uniforms, storefront signage, banners and other color-critical work.

The same locked artwork applies to business cards, letterheads, envelopes, invoices, folders, bags, packaging, stickers, labels, delivery packaging, employee ID cards, uniforms, signage, roll-up banners and event material.
