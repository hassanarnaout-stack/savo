# SAVO Packaging Identity Specifications

Supplier dielines and production dimensions are mandatory inputs and are not included here.

| Application | Identity guidance | Production checks |
|---|---|---|
| Shopping bag | Primary lockup on broad face; mark permitted on gusset | Handle zone, folds, ink coverage, stock proof |
| Delivery bag | High-contrast lockup; tamper area remains unobstructed | Seal, thermal/ink adhesion, recycling marks |
| Shipping box | Single-color lockup where process requires; preserve full-color master elsewhere | Corrugation, flexo gain, seam and tape zones |
| Product sticker | Mark or lockup according to available width | Adhesive, radius, cut tolerance, minimum size |
| Packaging seal | Standalone mark preferred | Closure stress, substrate, tamper behavior |
| Thank-you card | Approved lockup with placeholder message area | Stock, finish, bilingual copy approval |
| Tissue/sticker branding | Repeating mark only with approved spacing | Ink show-through and repeat registration |
| Tape branding | Repeated lockup/mark with clear space | Repeat length, seam visibility, ink adhesion |

Keep artwork away from cuts, creases, heat seals, zippers and barcodes. Dark or transparent substrates may require an approved white-ink underbase. Confirm recyclability, regulatory copy and local labeling separately; this identity document does not supply regulatory content.
